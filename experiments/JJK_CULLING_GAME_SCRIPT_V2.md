# Culling Game — original narration draft v2
Status: draft for canon review, not yet synthesized or rendered. Target ~60–70 seconds depending on delivery.

Imagine waking up with a cursed technique you never asked for. Now you've got nineteen days to enter a death game, or the technique gets ripped out of you. And that could kill you.

That's what Kenjaku set up across Japan: ten colonies, with awakened sorcerers and fighters reincarnated from centuries ago.

Here's why Yuji and Megumi can't just walk away. Megumi's sister, Tsumiki, is caught in it too. They need a way to save her.

Inside the colonies, fighting earns points. Defeat a sorcerer, five. A non-sorcerer, one. Reach a hundred, and you can propose a new rule. But you can't simply propose ending the game.

And hiding? That's not a safe plan either. Go nineteen days without your score changing, and the game's penalty comes for you.

Here's the twist: Kenjaku doesn't need a champion. The battles are part of a ritual gathering cursed energy for his plan involving Tengen.

So the real challenge isn't winning a tournament. It's finding a loophole before people die.

Now you know the rules. What new rule would YOU add? Mine: if you defeat a curse that's a player, you automatically claim its remaining points. Would the game even allow that?
