# Bouriko SFX sources

The pipeline uses downloaded stock sound effects instead of generating tones.

All effects below are from Mixkit and are identified as available under the
Mixkit Free License. Mixkit says its free sound effects can be used in
commercial and personal projects, including YouTube and social media, without
required attribution. Do not redistribute the raw files separately from a
finished creative project.
